# Beginner-Level Murder Mystery Design: The Velvet Lily Hotel

![Velvet Lily Hotel - Beginner](sandbox:/mnt/data/A_written_presentation_titled_"The_Velvet_Lily_Hot.png")

---

## Setting
**The Velvet Lily Hotel** — An eerie, vintage 1930s luxury hotel turned bohemian artist commune. New Year’s Eve. Guests are snowed in by a blizzard. The faded glamour hides deep secrets.

---

## The Crime
- **Victim:** Vivienne LaRue — A retired jazz singer, found dead in her penthouse bathtub, surrounded by rose petals and broken glass.
- **Official cause of death:** Suicide  
- **True cause:** Blunt force trauma

---

## Suspects & Witnesses

### 1. **Ezra Vale** – Concierge
- Secretly in love with Vivienne
- Hiding a criminal record
- Heard a noise during time of death but lied

### 2. **Claudette Rose** – Rival Starlet
- Jealous of Vivienne
- Claimed she was asleep
- Threatened Vivienne the day before

### 3. **Dr. Lionel Finch** – Psychiatrist
- Had private sessions with Vivienne
- Hid her journal
- **True murderer**

### 4. **Maribel Sweet** – Teenage Runaway
- Taken in by Vivienne
- Saw someone leave the suite but is too scared to speak

### 5. **Chef Anton** – Chef
- Overheard argument between Vivienne and another guest
- Protects Maribel

---

## Murder Plot
Dr. Finch murdered Vivienne to stop her from exposing his criminal past. He staged the scene to look like suicide, pulled the fire alarm to distract staff, and faked mirror-related injuries.

---

## Clue Paths *(Beginner Complexity)*

### 🔍 Interview Path
- Ezra mentions a crash he "forgot"
- Claudette slips about the argument
- Maribel hints she saw someone sneak out

### 🧰 Search Path
- Broken mirror has blood behind it
- Missing champagne bottle with blood found in Finch’s room
- Torn journal page in trash implicates “L.F.”

### 🧠 Logic Path
- Dr. Finch had access and motive
- Multiple witnesses say Vivienne wasn’t suicidal

---

## Resolution
Confront Dr. Finch using collected clues. He either confesses or tries to gaslight the player in a staged therapy session. Final choice: arrest him or let him manipulate further.

---

## Tone
Intriguing, theatrical, beginner-friendly with light suspense.
