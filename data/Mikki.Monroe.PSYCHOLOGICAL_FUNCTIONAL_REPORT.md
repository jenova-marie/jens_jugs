- Hypomanic states result in sleeplessness, impulsive productivity, and eventual burnout.

### 3. **Generalized Anxiety Disorder with Panic Features**

- Experiences full-body panic symptoms in high-stakes or unfamiliar settings (e.g. government offices, job interviews, family confrontations).

### 4. **Persistent Depressive Disorder (Dysthymia)**
- Chronic, low-grade depression lasting multiple years.
- Feels consistently worthless, disconnected, and “too broken to fix.”
- Struggles to derive joy or purpose from daily activities despite periods of insight.
